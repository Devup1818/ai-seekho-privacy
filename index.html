AI Seekho Uploader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

AI Seekho Uploader is a personal tool used by one person, its developer, to upload their own educational videos to their own YouTube channels (AI Seekho and AI Made Simple).

What the app accesses

With the developer's permission, the app uses the YouTube upload permission only to upload videos, thumbnails and titles to the developer's own channels.

What we collect and store

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or any other users. The sign-in token is kept privately on the developer's own computer and is never sent to anyone else.

Third parties

Data is sent only to Google/YouTube APIs, to upload the developer's own videos. It is not shared with anyone else.
